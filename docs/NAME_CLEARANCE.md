# Name, domain, and trademark clearance

“Open Mermaid” is a working project name, not a completed legal conclusion. This checklist records product and engineering inputs for qualified legal review; it is not legal advice and does not authorize launch.

## Before public launch

1. Identify the countries and markets where the project will be offered, promoted, funded, or advertised.
2. Search relevant trademark registries for exact, phonetic, translated, and related software/service marks.
3. Search company names, app stores, package registries, source hosts, social handles, search results, and domain history for confusingly similar use.
4. Review the upstream Mermaid project’s name, marks, license, brand guidance, and community expectations.
5. Have qualified counsel assess likelihood of confusion, descriptive/nominative use, domain risk, and the proposed independent visual identity.
6. Reserve only approved domains, package names, repository names, and handles; document owners and renewal controls.
7. Review the exact site header, favicon, Open Graph image, metadata, README, screenshots, and future advertising language together.
8. Publish a clear non-affiliation statement if counsel approves continued use.
9. Record the decision, reviewer, date, jurisdictions, limitations, and reevaluation trigger in a private project record.

## Engineering hold points

- Keep `PUBLIC_INDEX_SITE=false` and preview `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow` until clearance and production review are complete.
- Do not submit sitemaps, preload HSTS, announce a domain, print promotional material, or create paid ads before approval.
- Keep branding assets independent; do not copy the Mermaid project’s logo, typography, layout, or trade dress.
- Treat a rename as a supported release operation: centralize site strings, avoid branding in document schemas, and inventory domains, package metadata, local database names, cache keys, screenshots, policies, and structured data.

## Current disclosure

The public pages and README state that Open Mermaid is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Mermaid project or OpenAI. That disclosure reduces ambiguity but does not replace clearance.

Re-run review before entering a new market, activating advertising, creating a ChatGPT App, offering paid services, transferring ownership, or materially changing the brand.
