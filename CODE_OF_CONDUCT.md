# Open Mermaid community code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or technical choices.

We will work to make participation welcoming, accessible, and constructive in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Respect different experience levels, communication styles, and accessibility needs.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Protect private information and follow coordinated vulnerability disclosure.
- Focus on what is best for users and the project community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, insults, personal attacks, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Encouraging others to violate this code or retaliating against a reporter.
- Using project spaces to distribute malware, credentials, private diagrams, or exploit details outside the security process.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, project communication, events, and public spaces where a person represents Open Mermaid or its community.

## Reporting and enforcement

For an ordinary moderation concern, contact a maintainer privately. For a sensitive report when no other private project channel is available, open a [private GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/farshadf/OpenMermaid/security/advisories/new) titled **Conduct report** and do not include unrelated secrets or private diagram data.

Maintainers will protect a reporter’s privacy as far as practical, evaluate context, avoid conflicts of interest, and apply a proportionate response. Possible actions include a private correction, warning, temporary participation limit, removal of content, or permanent ban. Immediate threats may be escalated to the platform or appropriate authorities.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may be removed from moderation responsibilities.
